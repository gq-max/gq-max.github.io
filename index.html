01 / RESEARCH

Research interests.

I study multimodal intelligence from three complementary perspectives: understanding, reliability, and model adaptation.

01

Human-centric multimodal understanding

Understanding human behavior, visual evidence, and temporal dynamics in images and videos; building benchmarks that reveal where MLLMs fail to reason.

Visual reasoning · Video understanding
02

Multimodal safety & adversarial robustness

Studying transferable attacks, physical patches, and jailbreak evaluations across VLMs, video MLLMs, and vision-language-action models.

Transferability · Physical robustness
03

Efficient post-training & evaluation

Adapting multimodal models through QLoRA, supervised fine-tuning, and GRPO; designing targeted data, rewards, and safety–utility evaluations.

QLoRA · SFT · GRPO
